FAQ

Frequently Asked
Questions

What is your typical project timeline? ▼

Timelines depend on scope and complexity. Websites are made in less than a week, intermediate projects 2-4 weeks, and large enterprise engagements 1+ months. We provide a detailed timeline estimate during our initial discovery call.

What technologies do you work with? ▼

We work across React/Next.js, Vue.js, Angular, Node.js, Python/Django, Java, .NET, Flutter, React Native, AWS, Azure, GCP, Docker, Kubernetes, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, GraphQL, and more. We select the right stack for each project.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance and support? ▼

Yes — every engagement includes a maintenance and support phase. We offer SLA-backed support packages including 24/7 monitoring, security patching, performance tuning, and roadmap-driven feature development.

How do you handle project pricing? ▼

We offer both fixed-price (for well-scoped projects) and time-and-materials engagement models. Pricing is transparent and itemized — you'll know exactly what you're paying for at every step.

Do you sign NDAs and IP agreements? ▼

Absolutely. We have standard NDA and IP assignment documents ready to execute before any engagement begins. All intellectual property belongs to the client.

Can you work with our existing team? ▼

We frequently augment internal teams, work alongside in-house developers, or lead entirely standalone projects. Our flexible engagement model adapts to your team's structure and process.
